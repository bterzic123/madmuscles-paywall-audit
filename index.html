What this is

We went through MadMuscles the way a new user does, then held your paywall up against the market.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, and this spring we published what 78,000+ of their paywalls show: what converts, by region and category. We took the quiz, reached your paywall, tapped every plan and closed it to see what happens next. Then we compared each part of it with the report.

What follows is four ideas. Each one is a test you can run, and each one is built into a clickable prototype of your own paywall. Benchmarks are for Health & Fitness apps in the US, your biggest App Store market. In the report, the typical US Health & Fitness app turns 2.86% of paywall views into paid users within 30 days.

We did not redesign your paywall

Every screen in the prototype is yours: the photo, the three plan cards with POPULAR and BEST PRICE, FOREVER FIT, the orange Continue in its glow, your prices. Flip “Today” above the phone and you get the paywall exactly as it ships.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest.

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes first, because that is what wins most often in the report. Each one opens the prototype on its screen.

01

Open on your 6-month plan

Today the paywall opens with 1 MONTH at 9,99 € selected, the plan with the highest weekly price. Your BEST PRICE badge already sits on 6 MONTHS at 29,99 €, which works out at 1,25 € a week, half of monthly’s 2,50 €. Select it by default and say that saving out loud.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 · Health & Fitness: annual is pre-selected on 63.5% of paywalls and brings 62% of revenue. See the screen 59% of testsplan-duration tests that raised LTV · report finding
02

Offer a smaller first step on close

Tap X today and the paywall slides away to account creation, right after a loader that says “We prepare special offers for you”. A short sheet with your own 1-month and 3-month plans keeps the door open, with “No thanks” one tap away. No new price is needed.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026: only 4% of apps show a second paywall after close; it beats the main one in 45% of apps. See the screen ARPU +10–15%based on Adapty data
03

Put their answers in the headline

The quiz asks for a name, a goal, problem areas and how often they can train. Then the headline says “Get your personalized workout plan” to everyone. Read it back instead: “Your Build Muscle plan is ready”, with chest, arms and 3–4 workouts a week underneath.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026: only 2% of top paywalls use what the app knows about the user. See the screen CR +8–17%observed in winning A/B tests
04

Show your 4.4 where the price is

134K ratings at 4.4 on the US App Store, and reviews like “Beginner Friendly”, appear nowhere on the paywall. The review cards on the loader are gone by the time the price shows. One line of rating and one real quote fit above the plans.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026: only 27% of top paywalls show social proof. See the screen CR +10–30%observed in winning A/B tests
